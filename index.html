Armour Up Daily – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Armour Up Daily ("the app") is a private daily companion for Christians, for Scripture, prayer, journaling and building spiritual habits. It is developed by Mark Peterson ("I", "me"). This policy explains how the app handles your information.

In short
  • Everything you enter stays on your device.
  • I do not collect, receive, store, sell or share any of your data.
  • There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • You can erase everything at any time.

1. Information the app stores

To work, the app stores the following only on your device:

  • Your first name or nickname, and the focus areas and goals you choose
  • Journal entries, prayers, reflections and notes
  • Saved verses, verse notes and memory progress
  • Mood check-ins, practice and habit history, and focus and rescue sessions
  • App settings

Some of this, such as journal entries and mood, can be personal and relates to your religious beliefs. Because it never leaves your device, I have no access to it.

2. Information I collect

None. The app has no servers and does not send your information to me or to anyone else. It does not use analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking tools, and it does not create an account. All Scripture, prayers and content are built into the app, so it works fully offline.

3. Sharing

I do not sell, share or transfer your information, because I never receive it. If you choose to copy a verse or an accountability message and send it to someone, that happens through your device's own apps and under their terms.

4. Backups

On Android, the app is set up so that its data is not included in Google cloud backups or device-to-device transfers. On iPhone, app data may be included in your own device or iCloud backup, under Apple's terms. I cannot access those backups.

Because there is no account or server backup, data that is erased cannot be recovered.

5. Deleting your data

You are in full control. You can erase everything by:

  • Going to Profile → Reset all app data, or
  • Uninstalling the app.

Since I hold no data about you, there is nothing for me to delete on request.

6. Permissions and links

The app does not ask for access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or photos, and it does not send notifications. When you tap an external link, such as this privacy policy, it opens in your web browser, which is governed by that browser's and website's own privacy practices.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because it collects no information at all.

8. Security

Your data is protected by your device's own security, such as its screen lock and storage encryption. Keeping your device secure keeps your data secure.

9. Changes to this policy

If the app changes how it handles information, for example by adding an optional backup feature, I will update this policy and the date at the top before that change is released.
