Armour Up Daily – Support

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Armour Up Daily is a private daily companion for Scripture, prayer, journaling and spiritual habits, with guided help for moments of temptation, anxiety or discouragement.

Contact us

Email hustlebluprint@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 working days. Please include your phone model and what happened.

In the app, you can also go to Profile → Help & support → Contact us, which adds your app version automatically.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up, login or email. Open the app and begin.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your phone. Your journal, prayers, moods and practices never leave your device. We cannot see them, and nothing is uploaded.

Is my data backed up?

Not by the app. On Android, app data is left out of Google backups to keep it private. On iPhone, it may be included in your own iCloud device backup. If you delete the app or reset it, your data is gone for good.

How do I erase everything?

Go to Profile → Reset all app data. Uninstalling the app also erases everything.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. All Scripture, prayers and guided flows are built into the app.

Which Bible translation do you use?

The Berean Standard Bible (BSB), which is dedicated to the public domain.

What is Rescue?

A guided path for hard moments: breathe, read Scripture chosen for what you are facing, pray, take a concrete next step, and check in. Leaving early still counts as reaching for help.

Is this a replacement for counselling or professional help?

No. Armour Up Daily supports your walk with God, but it is not medical or mental health care. If you are in danger or thinking about harming yourself, contact your local emergency number or a crisis line straight away.

Why don't I get reminders?

Reminders are not in this version yet. They are planned for a future update.

My streak reset. Why?

Daily practices count consecutive days. If a day is missed, the streak begins again, and grace covers every day.

How do I suggest a feature or report a problem?

Email hustlebluprint@gmail.com with your phone model and what happened.
